## Authorship agreement

Each author signs below once the team agrees on all of these points:

1. **Who is an author.** Someone who made a substantial contribution to the concept, software,
   analysis or writing, *and* reviewed and approved what is released or submitted. Help that
   does not meet this bar goes in the acknowledgements.
2. **Order.** Ordered by size of contribution, as agreed by the team. Ties are decided by
   discussion; if there is no consensus, alphabetical order.
3. **Corresponding author.** Handles submissions, journal correspondence and data or code
   requests.
4. **Later contributors** (e.g. a faculty mentor) join by unanimous agreement of the current
   authors, with their position agreed at that time.
5. **AI tools are not authors.** Their use is disclosed instead (see [AI_USE.md](AI_USE.md)).
6. **Every author reviews and approves** each software release and each manuscript before it is
   made public or submitted.
7. **Leaving the project.** Contributions made so far are still credited. Authorship of future
   papers depends on meeting point 1 for those papers.
8. **Disputes** are settled by discussion first, then by a mentor or advisor all authors accept.
