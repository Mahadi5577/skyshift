# Mentor outreach email (template)

Send to 2-3 people at most, one at a time, each email personalized. Good targets: astronomy or
physics faculty at your university or a nearby one, researchers who have published on SPHEREx,
Solar System surveys or citizen science (search ADS for "SPHEREx asteroids" or "citizen science
asteroid"), or national astronomy outreach groups.

Attach the one-pager as a PDF and include a 60-90 s demo video link. Do not attach code.

---

**Subject:** Student project on moving objects in SPHEREx data: seeking a mentor

Dear Dr. _[Name]_,

I am _[name]_, a _[year/program]_ at _[institution]_. I read your _[specific paper or talk:
one sentence on why it relates]_, and I am writing to ask whether you would consider
mentoring a small student project that builds on public SPHEREx data.

We built SkyShift, an open-source tool that finds wavelength-matched SPHEREx exposures of any
sky position at time scales from hours to a year, and shows how the sky changes. On Quick Release 2
data we have:
- recovered (10) Hygiea moving 5 pixels in 1.6 hours and Pluto drifting ~3 pixels per day, and
- measured detection limits with planted synthetic movers (89% recovery at peak S/N 8).

We would like to turn this into a completeness study of SPHEREx's sensitivity to moving Solar
System objects. Your guidance on photometric calibration and on the statistics would make the
difference between a demo and a publishable result.

A one-page summary is attached, and there is a 90-second demo here: _[link]_. Would you have
20 minutes for a call in the next few weeks? I am also happy to answer questions by email.

Thank you for your time,
_[Name]_
_[Institution, email, ORCID]_

---

**If there's no reply after 10-14 days:** send one short, polite follow-up, then move on to the
next person.
